About JND

An IT services team built around two promises: reliable support and professional service.

Who we are

JND Integrated Solutions is an IT services business. We look after the technology a company runs on — the support its people need day to day, the network and servers underneath it, and the security and cameras around it.

Most of our clients are small and mid-sized businesses with no IT department of their own, or a small one that needs cover. They want someone who answers the phone, fixes the problem, and explains what happened in language that does not need a glossary.
