IT Consultancy & Project Implementation

The decisions about IT that have money and disruption attached to them, taken with something to base them on — and the project work that follows once you decide.

What this covers

Most decisions about IT are made without a clear picture of what is already there. Equipment is bought, contracts renew, and nobody is quite sure which parts still do a job and which are about to fall over. Looking at the whole picture first is cheaper than replacing the wrong thing.

We look at what is actually in place — the systems, the contracts, the licences and the equipment — and set it out in writing. From that you get a plan with priorities on it, so the urgent is separated from the expensive, and replacements are budgeted for rather than made in a hurry. We can then run the work through from start to finish, or hand it back to your own team.

What's included

What a consultancy engagement looks like in practice.

  • A review of the systems, contracts, licences and equipment you already have
  • A written plan with the work put in priority order
  • Budgeting and planning replacements instead of replacing things in a hurry
  • A project run end to end — a move, a new site, a major change — including the parts that are not IT
  • Working alongside your other suppliers, not around them
  • A second opinion on a quote or a proposal you have been given

How we work

The same four steps whether it is one laptop or a whole site.

  1. 1

    Tell us about it

    By phone, email or the form on the contact page. We confirm it has been picked up, and say who has it.

  2. 2

    We diagnose it

    Remote first where that is quicker, on site where it is not. Either way, you are told what we found.

  3. 3

    We fix it

    The fix, why it happened, and what to do if it comes back — in language that does not need a glossary.

  4. 4

    We stop it coming back

    Where the same fault keeps returning, we look at the cause rather than the symptom, and tell you what it would take.

Why it works

What you get out of having someone look at the whole picture.

A plan you can act on

The priorities are written down, with what is urgent kept apart from what is merely expensive.

Nothing bought in a hurry

Replacements are planned and budgeted for, so a failure does not force the decision on the worst possible day.

One plan, one owner

A move or a new site runs to a single plan that includes the parts which are not IT, so nothing falls between suppliers.

Questions we get asked

How is this charged?

We scope the work first, then put it in a written proposal, so you know what is covered before anything starts. The shape of that proposal depends on what you need — a one-off review, a plan, or a project run for you from start to finish.

Do we have to buy anything from you?

No. The advice stands on its own, and there is nothing you are obliged to buy. Where equipment or a service is needed you are free to buy it wherever you like, or to ask us to quote for it separately.

Can you pick up a project someone else started?

Yes, and it happens often. The first step is finding out what has actually been done, rather than what was planned. We then tell you plainly what can be kept, what needs redoing, and what it would take to finish.

Will you sit in on a meeting with our other suppliers?

Yes. It is often the quickest way to settle a question, and we are happy to join a call or come to a meeting. We will say what we think, including where we disagree with something you have been told.

What do you need from us to get started?

Access to the systems, the contracts and whatever paperwork exists, and someone on your side who can answer questions. Most of the work is ours; yours is mostly confirming that what we have found matches what you know.
