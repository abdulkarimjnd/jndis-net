IT Equipment Supply & Installation

The right hardware for the work your team actually does, supplied with the licences it needs, set up before it reaches a desk, and installed on site when there is more to it than a laptop.

What this covers

The equipment someone uses should follow from the work they do, not from whatever happens to be on a shelf. We look at the job first — the software, the screens, the moving about — and choose the machines that fit it. That covers laptops, desktops, monitors and the accessories around them.

Supply and setup are one job. The machine arrives with its licences in place, its accounts created and its settings and network access already configured, so nobody spends their first morning installing things. We hand it over to the person who will use it, and take the old equipment away.

What's included

What supplying and installing equipment looks like in practice.

  • Hardware chosen for the job it has to do, not taken off a shelf
  • Laptops, desktops, monitors and the accessories around them
  • Licences supplied and accounted for alongside the hardware
  • Machines set up, joined to the network and handed to their user
  • Larger installs as well — cabinets, cabling, access points and cameras
  • Old equipment removed, with the data wiped and the wipe confirmed to you

How we work

The same four steps whether it is one laptop or a whole site.

  1. 1

    Tell us about it

    By phone, email or the form on the contact page. We confirm it has been picked up, and say who has it.

  2. 2

    We diagnose it

    Remote first where that is quicker, on site where it is not. Either way, you are told what we found.

  3. 3

    We fix it

    The fix, why it happened, and what to do if it comes back — in language that does not need a glossary.

  4. 4

    We stop it coming back

    Where the same fault keeps returning, we look at the cause rather than the symptom, and tell you what it would take.

Why it works

What you get out of buying the hardware this way.

The right machine to begin with

We match the specification to the work, so nobody ends up fighting a computer that was never bought for the job.

Old equipment dealt with properly

What we take away has its storage wiped, and you are told what was done rather than left with a machine in a cupboard.

One thread from order to desk

Hardware, licences and setup sit with one team, so there is no gap between the box arriving and the machine being used.

Questions we get asked

How do you decide what to buy?

We start with the work: the software people depend on, the screens they need, how much they move about. Then we put the specification together and show you where the money is going, so the choice is yours and you can see why it was made.

Does somebody set it up, or does it arrive in a box?

It is set up before it reaches a desk. Accounts, licences, settings and network access are all configured, then the machine is handed to the person who will use it already working, with their data and mail in place.

What happens to the equipment you take away?

The storage is wiped before anything else happens to it, and you are told what was done rather than left guessing. You can keep the machine if you would rather, and we will hand it back cleared.

Can we buy from you and install it ourselves?

Yes. You can take the supply on its own and fit it however suits your team, or take the supply and the setup together. If you install it yourselves, we will still say what the machines are configured to expect.

Can you handle a whole office at once?

Yes. Beyond desks, that covers the cabinets, cabling, wireless access points and cameras a site needs, installed and left in a state you can hand to whoever looks after the building.
