Web Development & Digital Solutions

A website that says what the business does and loads on a phone in a yard or a van — built new or rebuilt, with the domain and hosting kept in your name.

What this covers

Most businesses come to us with one of two problems: a site built years ago that no longer says what the business does, or no site at all. We build new sites and rebuild tired ones, working from what you sell and who buys it rather than from a template. The layout is drawn for a small screen first, because that is where most people will first find you.

The practical parts matter as much as the look. Forms have to reach the right inbox rather than a folder nobody reads, and every page has to give search engines the titles, descriptions and headings they need before they can list it at all. Your domain and hosting stay in your name, and we agree what happens after launch, because a site starts going stale within a year.

What's included

What a web project covers, from first drawing to the day after launch.

  • A site that says what the business does and opens quickly on a phone
  • New builds, and rebuilds of sites that have gone stale
  • Layouts drawn for a small screen first, then widened for desktop
  • Forms and enquiry routes that reach the right inbox
  • Page titles, descriptions, headings and a sitemap, so search engines can find you
  • Domain and hosting kept in your name, with a plan for what happens after launch

How we work

The same four steps whether it is one laptop or a whole site.

  1. 1

    Tell us about it

    By phone, email or the form on the contact page. We confirm it has been picked up, and say who has it.

  2. 2

    We diagnose it

    Remote first where that is quicker, on site where it is not. Either way, you are told what we found.

  3. 3

    We fix it

    The fix, why it happened, and what to do if it comes back — in language that does not need a glossary.

  4. 4

    We stop it coming back

    Where the same fault keeps returning, we look at the cause rather than the symptom, and tell you what it would take.

Why it works

What you get out of having the site built this way.

It works on the phone in their hand

The layout is built for a small screen first, so a customer standing in a yard or sitting in a van can read it and call you from it.

Enquiries do not go missing

Every form is tested against the inbox it is meant to reach, so a message is not lost to a spam folder or a mistyped address.

You own what has been built

The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and the site is yours to move elsewhere if you ever want to.

Questions we get asked

Can you work with our existing website?

Often, yes. We can keep the parts that work and rebuild the parts that do not, or start again where the structure is holding you back. We will tell you which of those is worth doing once we have seen the site.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and the words and images on the site are yours as well. If you ever move to another supplier, everything is in your account rather than ours.

How do we update it ourselves?

Small changes — a price, a phone number, a new member of staff — can be made by your team through a simple editing page, and we will show you how. Where a change touches the layout or the code underneath, we make it for you so nothing else moves.

Do you write the words and take the pictures?

Either way. Many clients write the words themselves because they know the trade, and we shape them into pages. We can also write them for you, and we will advise on photographs — the good ones are usually taken at your own site rather than bought in.

What happens after the site goes live?

A site starts going stale within a year: prices change, staff move on, services come and go. We agree a routine before launch — what gets reviewed, how often, and who sends us the changes — so the site does not quietly fall out of date.
