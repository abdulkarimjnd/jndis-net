Network Infrastructure & Solutions

The cabling, switches, routers and wireless access points your business runs on — designed, installed, tidied up and written down, whether it is a new site or one you have inherited.

What this covers

A network that works is one you stop noticing. When it stops working, the fault is usually somewhere between the wall socket and the cabinet: a patch lead in the wrong port, an access point covering a corridor it cannot reach, a switch nobody has labelled.

We design and install the structured cabling, switches, routers and wireless access points, then keep them monitored and documented. If you have inherited a network from someone else, we start by mapping what is actually there, so the next fault is found from a record rather than worked out from scratch.

What's included

What a network project looks like, from the first socket to the paperwork.

  • Structured cabling and patching, run to the points you actually use
  • Switches, routers and wireless access points, configured and labelled
  • A wireless survey that finds the dead spots rather than guessing at them
  • Cabinets and patch panels tidied, labelled and put in order
  • Guest and visitor traffic kept off the network your business runs on
  • Monitoring and written documentation of what is where, so a fault is findable next time

How we work

The same four steps whether it is one laptop or a whole site.

  1. 1

    Tell us about it

    By phone, email or the form on the contact page. We confirm it has been picked up, and say who has it.

  2. 2

    We diagnose it

    Remote first where that is quicker, on site where it is not. Either way, you are told what we found.

  3. 3

    We fix it

    The fix, why it happened, and what to do if it comes back — in language that does not need a glossary.

  4. 4

    We stop it coming back

    Where the same fault keeps returning, we look at the cause rather than the symptom, and tell you what it would take.

Why it works

What you get out of having the network designed and recorded properly.

One network, not a patchwork

Cabling, switching, wireless and security sit with the same team, so nobody has to work out which piece is at fault before it can be fixed.

Coverage where you need it

Dead spots are found by survey rather than by moving a desk and hoping, so the wireless holds where your team actually works.

A map of what you have

Every cabinet, port and access point is labelled and written down, so the next fault is traced from a record rather than from scratch.

Questions we get asked

Will new cabling mean disruption while we work?

Some work has to happen during the day, and we plan around your hours where we can. Much of the cabling can be run outside working time, or in areas you are not using at the time. You are told what will be disturbed before anything is.

Our wi-fi drops out in parts of the building — can that be fixed?

Yes. A wireless survey measures the signal across the building and shows where it drops, which is usually a question of placement and channels rather than new hardware. We fix what the survey finds, then survey the result to confirm it holds.

Can you take over a network someone else installed?

Yes, and it is common. We start by mapping what is already there — the cabling, the switches and the addressing — then tell you plainly what is worth keeping and what is not.

Do you document what you install?

Yes. You get a written record of what was installed, where it sits and how it is labelled, along with the addressing and the wireless layout. It stays with you, so a problem can be looked up rather than worked out again.

Will we have to replace everything at once?

No. Where the existing cabling and switching will do the job, they stay. We set out what genuinely needs replacing and what can be kept, and the decision is yours.
