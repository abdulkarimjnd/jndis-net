Managed IT Services

The whole arrangement rather than a helpline: support, monitoring and someone who knows the estate, on one recurring agreement.

What this covers

IT support takes the calls. A managed agreement is the whole arrangement around them: support, monitoring and someone who knows how the estate is put together. What is covered is written down before you sign, rather than discovered afterwards.

It suits a business with no IT staff at all, and a business with a small team that needs cover and a second pair of hands. Either way the shape is the same — a plan you can read, scheduled visits, and a review of what is coming up next year.

What's included

What a managed agreement covers, month to month.

  • Everyday support by phone, email and remote session
  • Monitoring that reports on things nobody has complained about yet
  • A register of the machines, who has them and which licences are being paid for
  • Scheduled on-site visits, rather than only arriving when something breaks
  • A regular review of what is ageing, what is being renewed and what next year costs
  • A named contact, so nobody has to explain the setup again to a stranger

How we work

The same four steps whether it is one laptop or a whole site.

  1. 1

    Tell us about it

    By phone, email or the form on the contact page. We confirm it has been picked up, and say who has it.

  2. 2

    We diagnose it

    Remote first where that is quicker, on site where it is not. Either way, you are told what we found.

  3. 3

    We fix it

    The fix, why it happened, and what to do if it comes back — in language that does not need a glossary.

  4. 4

    We stop it coming back

    Where the same fault keeps returning, we look at the cause rather than the symptom, and tell you what it would take.

Why it works

What you get out of having the whole estate on one agreement.

One agreement, not a list of jobs

Support, monitoring, patching and the review sit together, so nothing has to be raised separately or argued over first.

Problems found before you feel them

Monitoring and the regular review turn up a disk that is filling or a licence nobody is using before either becomes urgent.

Someone who knows the estate

A named contact holds the register and the history, so you are not explaining your setup from the beginning each time.

Questions we get asked

How is this different from your IT support service?

IT support is the helpdesk: you report a problem and we work on it. Managed IT is that work plus the parts you would not think to ask for — monitoring, patching, a register of the estate and a review of what is coming up, on one recurring agreement.

What happens as we grow?

The agreement is reviewed as you grow. New starters, new sites and new equipment go onto the register, and the review is where we set out what that change does to the plan.

Is there a minimum term?

No. Ad-hoc support and a recurring agreement are both available, and plenty of clients start ad-hoc and move onto a plan once it is clear what the estate actually needs. What either arrangement includes is agreed before anything starts.

What is not included?

Things bought on your behalf, such as software licences and new hardware, and one-off projects. Those are quoted separately, so they appear on their own line rather than being hidden inside the agreement.

Who do we actually speak to?

A named contact, who knows the estate and its history. They are the person you raise things with, and the person who brings in the right engineer when a job needs one.
